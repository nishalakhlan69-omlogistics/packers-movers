Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our packing, moving, and relocation structures.

What items are excluded by Om Logistics and Packers?

We help you move everything from 1 RK to 5 BHK and villas. However, we do not deliver item(s) which are illegal, dangerous, flammable, or hazardous in nature or which are prohibited by law.

How safely do you pack goods?

Om Logistics utilises three-layer packing for items, including fragile and valuable goods. We ensure that packing is damage-proof using bubble wraps, wrapping paper, cardboard boxes, wrapping foam, and packing tapes.

How much do packers and movers charge?

Our charges are completely transparent. What you see is what you pay. Prices are subject to revision if there is a change in the destination location or your final item list details.

Why do the final charges vary from initial estimates?

Prices are subject to revision if there is a modification in the dropping location coordinates or total final inventory volumes before the operations launch.

Do you dismantle and assemble furniture?

Yes, we do complete dismantling and assembly of furniture wherever possible. Most furniture items do not need dismantling, but large modular beds and dining tables are disassembled to move safely.

Are my goods fully insured during transport?

We offer full safety management frameworks. If formal transit coverage is requested, comprehensive protection forms can be attached to the final booking memo easily.

What is the cheapest way to relocate?

Om Logistics offers complete house shifting solutions. But if you have already packed your goods and are ready to do some heavy lifting, you can book our cargo logistics trucks and save money.

Does Om Logistics provide intercity/interstate house shifting service?

Yes, we do provide intercity/interstate house shifting services safely linking major states across our centralized network.

Do you provide only labour or helper along with transport vehicles?

Yes, if you have already packed your items and need help with loading and unloading, you can book our cargo vehicles with helpers for straightforward loading and unloading transit.

Do packers and movers pack everything inside a household?

Yes, everything including fragile glass and valuables. Flammable products, chemical fluids, and gas cylinders are omitted from transport vehicles for protection.

How much does packing a complete house cost?

Packing costs depend directly on total layout room volume, quantity of goods, and total specialized packaging materials required to preserve fragile elements.

Are there any additional or hidden charges?

The quote includes all charges. No hidden fees unless there are last-minute changes to your inventory list.
