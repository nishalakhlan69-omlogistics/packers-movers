Frequently Asked Questions

Quickly access clear, transparent answers regarding our packing standards and insurance options.

What items are excluded by Om Logistics and Packers?

We help you move everything from 1 RK to 5 BHK and villas. However, we do not deliver items which are illegal, dangerous, flammable, hazardous, or prohibited by law (like gas cylinders, explosive items, or liquid fuels).

How safely do you pack goods?

Om Logistics utilizes a robust three-layer packing system. We ensure your items—including fragile glassware and high-value home appliances—are protected using premium air-bubble sheets, sturdy corrugated cartons, and stretch film wraps.

How much do packers and movers charge?

Our pricing model is completely transparent and computed upfront based on total packing volume, point-to-point distance, specific vehicle size required, and active labor headcount. There are zero hidden fees added on the delivery day.

Do you dismantle and assemble furniture?

Yes! Our professional moving crew handles the complete disassembling and reassembling of large furniture setups like modular beds, dining tables, and large wardrobes to guarantee completely scuff-free transport.

Are there any additional or hidden charges?

The quote provided by our coordinator after inventory verification covers all toll taxes, service components, and operational loops. No extra charges are added unless you choose to add extra goods or change target locations last-minute.
