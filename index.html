Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our packing, moving, and relocation structures.

What items are excluded by Om Logistics and Packers?

We help you move everything from 1 RK to 5 BHK and villas. However, we do not deliver items which are illegal, dangerous, flammable, hazardous, or prohibited by law (like gas cylinders or fuel fluids).

How safely do you pack goods?

Om Logistics utilizes a robust three-layer packing system. We ensure your items—including fragile glass and high-value electronics—are fully protected using premium bubble wraps, strong carton boxes, and stretch film.

How much do packers and movers charge?

Our pricing is completely transparent based on your specific packing volume, moving distance, vehicle type, and required labor force. There are no hidden fees, and you'll receive a clear breakdown upfront.

Do you dismantle and assemble furniture?

Yes! Our professional moving crew handles the complete disassembling and reassembling of large furniture items like modular beds, dining tables, and wardrobes to ensure they are transported safely without damage.

Are there any additional or hidden charges?

The quote we provide after assessing your inventory list includes all operational costs. No extra charges will be added unless you make last-minute modifications to your destination or item list details.
