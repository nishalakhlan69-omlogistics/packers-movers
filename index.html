Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I book my relocation request?

We recommend confirming your shifting request 3 to 5 days out to map packing resources and book containers seamlessly.

Do you charge additional fees for disassembly and reassembly?

No, standard basic breakdown and assembly of simple beds, office workstations, and standard closets are completely included in our core quotes.

Are my delicate goods protected during rain?

Yes, all long-haul transit is managed exclusively through completely enclosed, waterproof cargo container trucks.
